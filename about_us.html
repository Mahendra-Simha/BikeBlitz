How does BikeBlitz works?

In order to ship your bike from us the Customer needs to Follow these things:

  • Visit the warehouse with the motorcycle
  • Carry a copy of RC card and Insurance
  • Carry a copy of Identity Proof (like license card, voter ID etc.)
  • Our Staff will Cross verify the customer details like model name and manufacturer.
